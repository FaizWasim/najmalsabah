Energy Consultancy & Services

One of the core aims of Najma Alsabah FM is to play a leadership role in driving the sustainability initiatives of the UAE leadership. We, therefore, aligned our strategic objectives with sustainability to develop services that will benefit not only our customers but also the environment.

Energy Consultancy: We have a dedicated team for conducting energy audits and advising customers on ideal energy conservation methods. Najma Alsabah FM is accredited by the Dubai Government’s Regulatory & Supervisory Bureau (RSB) for Electricity and Water as an Energy Services Company (ESCO). Besides, our Energy Auditors and Measurement & Verification professionals are certified by US-based Association of Energy Engineers (AEE) and Efficiency Valuation Organisation (EVO) respectively. The provisional accreditation and certification is assurance that we have highly qualified personnel and robust financial status needed for delivering energy saving projects for both public and private sector clients.

Energy Performance Contracting: Najma Alsabah FM offers two unique propositions of energy performance contracting. In both models, we supply, install, commission and maintain Energy Conservation Mechanisms (ECM) at client sites, following an investment grade energy audit. Both the models offer energy savings, which will translate in the long-term to significant cost savings for property owners.

Engineering & Maintenance

One of our core competencies remains MEP services as we have an extensive team of experienced and well-qualified technicians. All our maintenance activities are geared towards ensuring proper functioning of equipment and systems, reducing their deterioration and avoiding downtime, which can otherwise have serious financial repercussions on the clients.

Through Planned Preventive Maintenance, we substantially reduce reactive maintenance and ensure the longevity of built assets. Condition-monitored maintenance is enabled by CAFM. We execute the work in a safe, efficient and unobtrusive manner to suit the convenience of the building’s tenants and visitors. Our PPM activities include:

  • Regular examination, inspection, lubrication, testing and adjustments of equipment
  • Use of non-destructive testing techniques such as vibration analysis, infrared testing and oil analysis to curb equipment breakdowns.
  • Reactive repairs in emergencies
