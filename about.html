Company Profile

Najma Alsabah FM’s progression in becoming a fully fledged Facilities Management service provider has been achieved over 44 years of strategic planning and development. Starting in 1974 as a mechanical contractor, we initially built our expertise in the installation and maintenance of mechanical systems.

In subsequent decades, through joint ventures, acquisitions and entrepreneurship, we broadened our horizon to include electrical systems, servicing of large tonnage chillers, general and specialised cleaning services, security services and most recently energy management by achieving RSB Accreditation as an Energy Services Company (ESCO).

Today, we have a strong and integrated service portfolio and are one of the largest FM service providers in the UAE. With 300+ live projects, we are leaders in infrastructure, luxury property, industrial facilities, mixed-use developments, commercial, residential, retail and education sectors.

Being one of 34 companies that forms the Najma Alsabah Group, our services are complemented by our sister companies which bring together a wealth of expertise across the industries. Well-defined knowledge management systems enable synergies that bring long-term benefit to our service delivery and, consequently, our customers.

Our extensive expertise comes together and enriches a knowledge-driven operation resulting in exceptional service. Our holistic approach to building management focuses on the complete lifecycle of the building. Our core FM activities are structured around: Strategic Facilities Planning (SFP), Predictive/Preventive Maintenance and Energy Management and cover MEP, hospitality, cleaning, security and specialised services.

At Najma Alsabah , we are passionate about the UAE’s vision to become a smart and sustainable country. Keeping up with the implementation speed is an exciting challenge for us and one we have taken on seriously. We are subscribed to the best of global innovations and international standards so that we can offer best-in-class services to our clients here in the UAE. We are one of the founding members of MEFMA and are accredited by several reputed industry associations.

CEO’s Message

Dr. NAVIN VALRANI

CEO, Najma Alsabah Facilities Management

Vice Chairman and Group Managing Director, Oasis Investment Co LLC

Najma Alsabah FM was created with a vision to provide customers in the UAE with world-class facilities services. The essence of the entire organisation breathes this philosophy and energises itself around our company slogan, “Experience the Difference”. Our commitment revolves around this philosophy and it is his commitment that gives facility owners and managers the confidence to use Najma Alsabah as opposed to international FM players that operate in this market.

Najma Alsabah FM’s technical pedigree is second to none. The organization’s technical pedigree can be traced back to 1974, when Najma Alsabah first ventured into the mechanical contracting business. Through the knowledge pool that Najma Alsabah developed in the air conditioning industry, it added electrical and plumbing services to its foray and in 2005, Najma Alsabah moved to being full-fledged facilities services provider.

Najma Alsabah FM’s technical pedigree is backed by the world’s finest IT systems. Najma Alsabah FM is an integral part of the Najma Alsabah Group of Companies, a $1 billion plus group that has been operating in the region since 1971. In today’s volatile environment, you can be rest assured that when you are dealing with Najma Alsabah FM, you are dealing with an organization that will be around for decades to come.

Group Profile

Oasis Investment Company LLC (OIC), the holding company of the Najma Alsabah Group of Companies, was founded in Dubai in 1971, by two visionary leaders – Abdulla Najma Alsabah , Founding Chairman and Mohan Valrani, Founding Senior Vice Chairman. From its humble beginnings as a trading and contracting company, the Group has grown into one of the largest private business conglomerates in the UAE. The reigns have now been successfully passed on to the second generation of leaders.

Today, the Najma Alsabah Group has 34 companies in diverse business sectors. Each company is an independent profit center, able to draw on the unrivalled in-house resources and technical expertise of the Group’s complementary businesses for its success. With an annual turnover of USD 1 Billion, Najma Alsabah has earned its place as a global player.
